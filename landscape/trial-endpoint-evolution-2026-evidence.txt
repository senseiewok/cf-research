# Evidence Corpus for Trial Endpoint Evolution Dossier (2026)

Section 1: PRISMA 2020 Flow and Identification
In the ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 CF snapshot, 1,053 studies were identified through the condition query.
Screening applied deterministic scope rules X1 through X6, resulting in 113 excluded studies.
Specifically, 72 studies were excluded under rule X4 because the condition list does not name cystic fibrosis.
41 studies were excluded under rule X5 because the condition list names cystic fibrosis only in a negation.
940 interventional studies were eligible and included in the trial endpoint atlas.
Among included studies, 916 studies registered primary outcomes and 24 studies had no registered primary outcome.
In total, 1,821 primary outcome entries were evaluated across the 940 included studies.

Section 4: Era Transition Matrix
In the pre-2012 era prior to ivacaftor approval, 56 entries evaluated lung_function_fev1, 24 evaluated nutritional_bmi, and 2 evaluated sweat_chloride.
In the 2012-2019 modulator expansion era, 82 entries evaluated lung_function_fev1, 47 evaluated quality_of_life, and 11 evaluated sweat_chloride.

Section 5: Benchmark Trials and Biophysical Standards
Ramsey BW et al. 2011 reported that ivacaftor in subjects with G551D showed significant improvements in percent predicted FEV1 in STRIVE (NCT00909532).
Wainwright CE et al. 2015 reported lumacaftor/ivacaftor in F508del homozygous subjects in TRAFFIC (NCT01807923) and TRANSPORT.
Davies JC et al. 2013 reported ivacaftor in pediatric subjects aged 6 to 11 years in ENVISION (NCT00909727).
Middleton PG et al. 2019 demonstrated that elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor in AURORA-44 significantly improved percent predicted FEV1 and CFQ-R respiratory scores.
Farrell PM et al. 2017 established sweat chloride concentration guidelines with a diagnostic threshold of 60 mmol/L.
Quittner AL et al. 2005 established that the minimal clinically important difference for the CFQ-R respiratory domain is 4.0 points.
Fuchs HJ et al. 1994 established standardized criteria for protocol-defined pulmonary exacerbations.
NCT05668741 BEACON-CF evaluated inhaled mRNA VX-522 before termination due to delivery and tolerability hurdles.
NCT05033080 SKYLINE 102 evaluated vanzacaftor/tezacaftor/deutivacaftor in subjects heterozygous for F508del and a minimal function mutation.
