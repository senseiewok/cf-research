In the 2024 Annual Data Report, eligibility for the newly approved CFTR modulator, VTD, is not included as it received FDA approval on December 20, 2024. As a result, unless it is explicitly stated in the text, individuals who are solely eligible for VTD (and not other modulators) are categorized as modulator ineligible in 2024 for the purposes of all charts and analyses in this edition.
On December 20, 2024, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) expanded the availability of ETI to include additional responsive variants in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) gene based on in vitro data. The label extension encompassed 799 individuals in the Registry, 375 of which were not previously eligible for any CFTR modulator therapies. Also on this date, the FDA granted initial approval to VTD for CF patients aged 6 years and older with at least one F508del mutation or another responsive CFTR mutation, including 31 additional rare mutations newly eligible under this approval. With this approval, 135 individuals in the Registry became eligible for a CFTR modulator for the first time. Overall, 92.3 percent of the total CF population in the Registry was eligible by CFTR genotype for at least one CFTR modulator as of 2024. In 2024, 26,277 of the 29,314 (89.6 percent) people eligible for a CFTR modulator based on age and genotype had at least one prescription reported in the Registry. The number of eligible individuals who were not prescribed a CFTR modulator fell from 3,065 (10.8 percent) in 2023 to 3,037 (10.4 percent) in 2024.
