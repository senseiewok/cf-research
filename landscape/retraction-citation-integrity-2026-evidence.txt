Section 1: Regulatory Label Provenance Across openFDA and EMA Medicines Catalogs
Following the 03/2026 label update, TRIKAFTA is indicated in patients aged 2 years and older who have a clinical diagnosis of CF and at least one variant that is responsive based on clinical and/or in vitro data (see Table 6) or results in production of CFTR protein.
Following the 03/2026 label update, ALYFTREK is indicated in patients 6 years of age and older who have a clinical diagnosis of CF and at least one variant that is responsive based on clinical and/or in vitro data (see Table 5) or results in production of CFTR protein.
EMA Kaftrio indications cover patients aged 6 years and older and paediatric patients aged 2 to less than 6 years who have at least one non-Class I mutation in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) gene.
EMA Alyftrek indications cover people aged 6 years and older who have at least one non-Class I mutation in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) gene.
On December 20, 2024, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration approved VTD for patients aged 6 years and older with at least one F508del mutation or another responsive CFTR mutation, including 31 additional rare mutations, and 135 individuals in the Registry became eligible for a CFTR modulator for the first time.
Clinical efficacy data for homozygous Class I nonsense genotypes without CFTR protein production are not stated in the 03/2026 openFDA TRIKAFTA or ALYFTREK prescribing information labels.
