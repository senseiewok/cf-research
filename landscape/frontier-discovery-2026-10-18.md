## 2. Thread 1: Mechano-Acoustic Mucus Conditioning & Epithelial Mechanotransduction

### 2.1 Observed Primary Evidence (Tier T2)
1. **Thixo-viscoelastoplastic constitutive behavior of human mucus:** Figueroa-Landeta et al. (2025), *"Thixo-viscoelastoplastic rheological characterisation of human mucus using a multimode BMP constitutive equation approach"*, DOI `10.1038/s41598-025-16178-z` (PMID: 41388033), characterizes human airway mucus as a thixotropic viscoelastoplastic material whose structural entanglement parameter $\lambda$ breaks down when oscillatory shear stress exceeds the mucus yield stress and recovers over a finite thixotropic relaxation window.
2. **Acoustic micro/nanobubble mucolytic conditioning:** Diane, Hudock, Leonard-Duke et al. (2026), *"Micro/nanoscale bubble-mediated mucolytic conditioning of the lung model of cystic fibrosis enhances nanoparticle transport through the mucus"*, DOI `10.1016/j.biomaterials.2026.124585` (PMID: 42702120), demonstrates in a CF lung mucus model that micro/nanoscale bubble conditioning disrupts mucin mesh hindrance and increases transmucosal nanoparticle transport.
3. **Clinical oscillatory vest rehabilitation:** ClinicalTrials.gov record `NCT07175012`, *"ELECTROVEST: Electro-Stimulation Vibratory Vest for Pulmonary Rehabilitation in Patients With Chronic Respiratory Diseases"* (completed, 21 participants, updated 2025-09-16), evaluated vibratory vest rehabilitation (`ELECTROVEST` and `The Vest Model 105`) with primary outcomes of expectorated sputum volume and discomfort score.

### 2.2 Computed Biophysical Results & In Silico Hypothesis
* **Computed (`tools/discovery/mechano_acoustic_delivery.py`):**
  - In unconditioned CF sputum ($7.5\%$ solids, yield stress $\tau_y = 3.400\text{ Pa}$, storage modulus $G' = 19.46\text{ Pa}$, mesh pore diameter $\xi = 58.5\text{ nm}$), a $72\text{ nm}$ polysarcosine-shielded SORT-LNP experiences severe steric hindrance ($D_{\text{eff}} = 8.17 \times 10^{-14}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}$), resulting in $0.0\%$ diffusive arrival across a $150\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ sputum blanket within a $30\text{ min}$ mucociliary clearance window.
  - Applying $15\text{ Hz}$ HFCWO oscillatory shear ($4.8\text{ Pa}$) reduces the BMP structural parameter to $\lambda = 0.649$ ($G'_{\text{eff}} = 9.74\text{ Pa}$, $\xi_{\text{eff}} = 73.3\text{ nm}$) for a $25.3\text{ min}$ thixotropic window and stimulates mechanosensitive ATP release ($238\text{ nM}$), raising predicted ASL height from $3.39\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ to $6.84\text{ }\mu\text{m}$.
  - Combining $20\text{ Hz}$ oscillatory acoustic shear ($8.5\text{ Pa}$) with microbubble conditioning ($I_{\text{cav}} = 0.65$) lowers $\lambda$ to $0.422$ ($G'_{\text{eff}} = 4.89\text{ Pa}$), widens mucin pores to $112.5\text{ nm}$ for $14.7\text{ min}$, restores ASL height to $7.60\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ ($408\text{ nM}$ ATP, $84.7\%$ `P2Y2`/`TMEM16A` activation), and increases $72\text{ nm}$ SORT-LNP diffusivity $24.38$-fold ($D_{\text{eff}} = 1.99 \times 10^{-12}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}$, lifting epithelial arrival from $0.0\%$ to $28.5\%$).
* **Our Inference (Hypothesis Badge — Not Clinical Guidance):** Pre-conditioning the airway with synchronized oscillatory acoustic shear ($15–20\text{ Hz}$) and microbubble mucolytics immediately prior to nebulizing stealth genetic nanoparticles could exploit the $15–25\text{ min}$ thixotropic pore-widening window and `PIEZO1`/`P2Y2`-mediated periciliary rehydration to reduce upper-mucus LNP entrapment and macrophage TLR activation.

## 3. Thread 2: Airway Basal Stem Cell Niche (`KRT5+`/`TP63+`) & Ionocyte Fluid Balance (`T-0220`)

### 3.1 Observed Primary Evidence (Tier T2)
1. **CFTR-rich pulmonary ionocytes:** Plasschaert et al. (2018), *"A single-cell atlas of the airway epithelium reveals the CFTR-rich pulmonary ionocyte"*, DOI `10.1038/s41586-018-0394-6`, and Yuan et al. (2025), *"Ionocyte CFTR Coordinates Chloride Absorption and Secretion to Balance Airway Fluid"*, DOI `10.1164/rccm.202505-1242oc` (PMID: 40815683), establish that rare `FOXI1+` pulmonary ionocytes regulate airway surface fluid balance alongside secretory cells and are continuously replenished from the basal progenitor pool.
2. **Ex vivo airway basal cell engraftment:** Vijaykumar et al. (2026), *"Basal cell transplantation supports multilineage differentiation and restores CFTR function in ex vivo ferret airways"*, DOI `10.1172/jci.insight.206375` (PMID: 42611556), demonstrates that transplanted basal stem cells engraft, differentiate into multilineage epithelia, and restore CFTR-dependent ion transport in ex vivo ferret airways.
3. **Extracellular vesicle delivery to airway basal cells:** Ivleva et al. (2026), *"Extracellular vesicle-based delivery to airway basal cells for durable gene therapy in cystic fibrosis"*, DOI `10.3389/fbioe.2026.1855907` (PMID: 42529376), evaluates extracellular vesicle (EV) tropism toward airway basal progenitor cells for durable CF gene correction.

### 3.2 Computed Persistence Kinetics (`tools/discovery/basal_stem_persistence.py`)
* **Computed (`T-0220`):**
  - Solving the three-compartment lineage ODE hierarchy ($B \to T \to D$, with luminal differentiated half-life $t_{1/2} = 100\text{ days}$, $\mu_D = 0.00693\text{ d}^{-1}$) via 4th-order Runge-Kutta confirms exact mass-balance conservation at homeostatic symmetric renewal ($p_s = 0.50$, $B_0 = 30.0$, $T^* = 10.0$, $D^* = 28.8539\text{ cells/unit}$).
  - **Episomal mRNA-LNP (`MOD-MRNA-LNP`):** Starting at $34.0\%$ WT CFTR rescue on Day 0, intracellular mRNA decay ($t_{1/2} = 3.5\text{ d}$) requires redosing every **$6.2\text{ days}$** to stay above the $10.0\%$ WT threshold ($0.0\%$ at Year 1).
  - **Episomal AAV Capsid (`MOD-AAV-EPISOMAL`, `NCT05248230`):** Starting at $43.0\%$ WT CFTR rescue on Day 0 ($35\%$ luminal + $8\%$ basal episomal), epithelial shedding and mitotic dilution reduce rescue to $10.0\%$ at **$203.2\text{ days}$** and $3.2\%$ at Year 1, requiring biannual redosing that faces anti-capsid neutralizing antibody (`NAb`) barriers (`T-0198`).
  - **Integrative / Genome-Edited Basal Stem Cells (`MOD-EV-BASAL-PE`, `MOD-PLASTICITY-PULSE`, `MOD-BASAL-ENGRAFT`):** Achieving $18.0\%$, $22.0\%$, or $28.0\%$ stable `KRT5+`/`TP63+` basal cell chimerism locks in an asymptotic 10-year homeostatic floor of **$18.0\%$**, **$22.0\%$**, and **$28.0\%$ WT CFTR** respectively (`DURABLE_HOMEOSTATIC_CURE`, zero redosing required).
