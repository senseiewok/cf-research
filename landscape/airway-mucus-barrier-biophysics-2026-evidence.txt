# Evidence Corpus for Airway Mucus Barrier Biophysics & Nanoparticle Transit Benchmark (2026)

Section 1: Hybrid Lipid-Polymer Nanoparticles and Gene Editing across the CF Airway Mucus Barrier
Conte G et al. 2022 reported hybrid lipid-polymer nanoparticles for pulmonary nucleic acid delivery with hydrodynamic diameters of 80 to 120 nm and dense polyethylene glycol surface shells (DOI: 10.1021/acsami.1c14975).
Dense polyethylene glycol shells with near-neutral zeta potential between -5 mV and +2 mV shield nanoparticles from electrostatic adhesion to negatively charged mucin fibers in cystic fibrosis sputum.
Comegna M et al. 2021 demonstrated base editing restoration of CFTR function in human cystic fibrosis airway epithelial models, highlighting that non-viral carriers must traverse the viscoelastic mucus layer before reaching apical epithelial cells (DOI: 10.1038/s41598-021-85549-z).
Alton EWFW et al. 2024 evaluated F/HN-pseudotyped rSIV.F/HN lentiviral vectors for cystic fibrosis airway gene transfer, demonstrating apical transduction of ciliated and basal airway epithelial cells in preclinical models (DOI: 10.1183/13993003.01683-2023).
In vivo mucociliary transit clearance half-life values in human patients for the Conte G et al. 2022 formulation are not stated in the in vitro study record.

Section 2: Renkin-Flory-Stokes-Einstein Screening and 5-Regime CF Sputum MPT Benchmark (T-0941 / PRED-0004)
In the Renkin-Flory-Stokes-Einstein airway mucus barrier screener (`tools/discovery/mucus_barrier_screener.py`), mucociliary clearance sweeps the periciliary airway surface within a reference window of 900 seconds (15 minutes), and in vivo clearance, spatial sputum heterogeneity, airway impaction, and clinical efficacy are explicitly listed under not_modeled_in_vivo.
Untreated purulent cystic fibrosis sputum has an average mucin and extracellular DNA mesh pore size of 140 nm, whereas recombinant human DNase (dornase alfa) pretreatment expands the effective mesh pore size to 220 nm.
Subtracting the untreated sputum mesh size of 140 nm from the dornase alfa pretreated mesh size of 220 nm yields an 80 nm increase in effective mesh pore diameter.
Across the 5-regime CF sputum multiple-particle-tracking (MPT) benchmark in mucus_barrier_screener.py, all 5 simulated carrier regimes matched their target diffusivity ratio bounds and expected verdicts (5 of 5 passed, 3 of 3 falsification controls passed):
Regime 1 (mpp_dense_peg_80nm): In mucus_barrier_screener.py, a simulated 80 nm dense-PEG mucus-penetrating nanoparticle with -2.5 mV zeta potential in 140 nm sputum across a 25 um mucus layer yields simulated D_eff/D_w of 0.052478 (target bounds 0.02 to 0.15) and simulated transit time of 724.84 seconds, classified as FEASIBLE_MUCUS_PENETRATING_CANDIDATE.
Regime 2 (mpp_hybrid_lipid_polymer_100nm): In mucus_barrier_screener.py, a simulated 100 nm dense-PEG hybrid lipid-polymer nanoparticle with -3.0 mV zeta potential in 140 nm sputum across a 15 um distal airway mucus layer yields simulated D_eff/D_w of 0.023324 (target bounds 0.01 to 0.08) and simulated transit time of 733.90 seconds, classified as FEASIBLE_MUCUS_PENETRATING_CANDIDATE.
Regime 3 (aav_capsid_25nm_neutral): In mucus_barrier_screener.py, a simulated 25 nm AAV capsid with -4.0 mV zeta potential in 140 nm sputum across a 25 um mucus layer yields simulated D_eff/D_w of 0.192784 (target bounds 0.15 to 0.30) and simulated transit time of 61.66 seconds, classified as FEASIBLE_MUCUS_PENETRATING_CANDIDATE.
Regime 4 (uncoated_cationic_lipoplex_110nm): In mucus_barrier_screener.py, a simulated 110 nm uncoated cationic lipoplex with +30.0 mV zeta potential and 0 percent PEG coverage in 140 nm sputum across a 25 um mucus layer yields simulated D_eff/D_w of 0.000054 (target bounds 0.0 to 0.005) and simulated transit time of 975498.44 seconds, classified as ELECTROSTATICALLY_TRAPPED_ON_MUCIN_FIBERS.
Regime 5 (oversized_conventional_particle_300nm): In mucus_barrier_screener.py, a simulated 300 nm conventional particle with -3.0 mV zeta potential in 140 nm sputum across a 25 um mucus layer (300 divided by 140 equals 2.14 diameter-to-pore ratio) yields simulated D_eff/D_w of 0.0 (target bounds 0.0 to 0.0) and infinite simulated transit time (None, sterically blocked), classified as STERICALLY_TRAPPED_BY_SPUTUM_MESH.
