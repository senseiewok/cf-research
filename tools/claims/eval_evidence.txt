=== Cystic Fibrosis Foundation Patient Registry (cffpr-txt) ===
Overall, 92.3 percent of the total CF population in the Registry was eligible by CFTR genotype for at least one CFTR modulator as of 2024.
In 2024, 26,277 of the 29,314 (89.6 percent) people eligible for a CFTR modulator based on age and was the most highly prescribed CFTR modulator.
The number of eligible individuals who were not prescribed a CFTR modulator fell from 3,065 (10.8 percent) in 2023 to 3,037 (10.4 percent) in 2024.

=== UK Cystic Fibrosis Registry (ukcfr-txt) ===
11,381 people with CF are registered within the UK CF Registry of whom 93% had annual review in 2024
8,830 people with CF with a 2024 annual review have at least one record of using a CFTR modulator in 2024 (Section 1.35a).
