## Positive control

2. **Exact 4-Point Collinear $3.8\text{ \AA}$ Chain Analytic Control**: on four collinear $C_\alpha$ atoms at $(3.8(k-1), 0, 0)\text{ \AA}$ for `auth_seq_id` $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, non-covalent ($|j - i| > 1$) compliance reproduces the exact rational vector $s_{\text{flex}} = [36/49,\, 4/5,\, 4/5,\, 36/49]$.

4. **Exact $SE(3)$ Rigid-Motion Invariance, Scale-Invariance of Rank & `train` Control**: applying a proper rigid motion $(R, \mathbf{t}) \in SE(3)$ to `5UAK` preserves $s_{\text{flex}}$ across all `1,139` placed residues within `< 1e-12` (and `Fraction(0)` under $SO(3, \mathbb{Q}) \ltimes \mathbb{Q}^3$ in `independent_audit.py`), and `ICL1` `G178` (`TRIKAFTA=1` in `train`) strictly outscores `TM1` `G85` in `train` ($s_{\text{flex}}(178) > s_{\text{flex}}(85)$).
