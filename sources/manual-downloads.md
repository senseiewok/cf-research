## Ask first (access by request)

- **WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (ICTRP) search portal web service** (World Health Organization): [page](https://www.who.int/tools/clinical-trials-registry-platform) - needs permission or an application; do not automate.

## Do not download

- **PubPeer (post-publication comments on papers)** (The PubPeer Foundation): [page](https://pubpeer.com/) - the publisher refuses automated access.
- **ClinGen (Clinical Genome Resource) curation of CFTR and other genes** (ClinGen consortium): [page](https://clinicalgenome.org/) - the publisher refuses automated access.
- **Global Variome shared LOVD - CFTR** (Leiden Open Variation Database): [page](https://databases.lovd.nl/shared/genes/CFTR) - the publisher refuses automated access.
